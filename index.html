Practice Campaign

Meta Lead Generation

Facebook & Instagram Ads

Objective: Generate enquiries for an education/service-based business.

View Case Study

Target Audience

Parents and working professionals aged 24–45 within a defined local area, reached through interest and behavior targeting around skill development, tutoring and professional courses rather than broad demographics alone.

Ad Concept

A short-form video and static carousel highlighting the course benefits, paired with one clear call-to-action ("Enquire Now") rather than a hard sell.

Campaign Structure

A cold-audience awareness set and a separate warm-audience retargeting set, using Meta's Lead Ads format to keep the enquiry process on-platform and reduce friction.

Lead-Generation Strategy

Used Meta's native lead form to capture name, contact number and area of interest, keeping the question set short to protect completion rates.

Key Learning

Keeping the lead form short and the ad message focused on one clear offer made the campaign far easier to test and evaluate than promoting multiple services at once.

Practice Campaign

Google Search Ads

Google Search Network

Objective: Reach people actively searching for a service.

View Case Study

Keyword Research

High-intent search terms tied directly to the service, including branded, generic and "near me" style local-intent keywords, with negative keywords to filter out irrelevant traffic.

Search Intent

Prioritized transactional and commercial-intent searches (e.g. "book", "price", "near me") over purely informational searches, since these sit closer to a buying decision.

Ad Copy

Built around the specific service and a clear value proposition, testing more than one headline and description to see what resonated with searchers.

Campaign Structure

Tightly themed ad groups so each group's keywords, ad copy and landing page stayed closely aligned, supporting relevance and Quality Score.

Conversion Strategy

Traffic directed to a landing page matching the ad's promise, with a simple enquiry form or click-to-call as the primary conversion action.

Key Learning

Tight keyword-to-ad-group alignment made it much easier to see which specific search terms were relevant, rather than mixing several services under one broad group.

Sample Project

Social Media Advertising Strategy

Meta Ads Strategy

A small service-based business had a social presence but no structured paid strategy — posts reached existing followers but not new customers.

View Case Study

Target Audience

Local and nearby audiences matching the business's typical customer profile, layered with interest-based targeting relevant to the service category.

Proposed Strategy

A phased approach — an awareness campaign to introduce the business to a wider relevant audience, an engagement phase, then a lead-generation phase for warmed-up audiences.

Creative Direction

Simple, benefit-led creative with a consistent visual style and one clear message per ad, rather than trying to say everything at once.

Lead Journey

Ad impression → landing page or lead form → enquiry → follow-up, with each step designed to reduce friction and drop-off.

Optimization Plan

Review performance at the ad-set level after an initial testing period, pausing underperforming audiences or creatives and reallocating budget toward what shows early promise.
